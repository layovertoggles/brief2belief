about

Portraits, reportage, behind the scenes, products, street (the banal kind), conceptual work, landscapes. Pretty much everything.

Finding my spot, staying out of the way, adapting to whatever the room throws at me. Natural light preferred, but sometimes light has to be added and lux measured to get the job done. Conditions read in advance, plans made around them.

Interesting people. Interesting situations. Colour dominants. Contrasts of meaning and shape. Searching for the perfect composition, one that says something. Mastering perfection is a must, but breaking the rules is always a challenge.

Emotions made to order for commercial work. Reportage is shot as found.

Briefs welcome. Beliefs optional.
